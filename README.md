# EableAI
An agentic AI for education that explains words in context, keeps learning focused, and organizes your academic life in one place.

**Your personal AI agent for focused, distraction-free learning.**

EableAI brings everything a student needs into one intelligent workspace. It understands words and concepts *in context* rather than giving dictionary definitions, and its learning-focused AI stays on academic tasks instead of becoming a shortcut. It also includes the tools that keep studying on track: a to-do list, a study tracker, and a calendar.

### Features
- **Contextual understanding:** explains words and ideas based on how they're used in your material
- **Academic-focused AI:** built to support learning, not replace it
- **Task management:** a to-do list made for coursework and deadlines
- **Study tracking:** see where your time goes and how you're progressing
- **Integrated calendar:** classes, exams, and study sessions in one view
